openExpress

Manage your Garmin watch on Linux

An unofficial app for Garmin watches and bike computers: sync with Garmin Connect, software updates, maps, Connect IQ apps and music, over USB.

Unofficial and independent: not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Garmin.

Install Source on GitHub

Install downloads a file that opens in GNOME Software. Or in a terminal:

flatpak install --user https://flatpak.bojanowski.me/io.github.tomaszbojanowski.OpenExpress.flatpakref
Or add the repository 
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists flathub https://dl.flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists bojanowski https://flatpak.bojanowski.me/bojanowski.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user bojanowski io.github.tomaszbojanowski.OpenExpress

Connect IQ apps

Install, update and remove apps, turn them on or off and put them back in order – also on watches that hide their app folder, such as the fēnix 8. Apps you added in the Connect IQ store wait on the same page.

Software updates

Updates for the device, downloaded from Garmin and checked before they are written to it.

What it does

  • Sync with Garmin Connect

    New activities, sleep, HRV and other data go up; courses, workouts and apps queued in Garmin Connect come down to the watch. TrueUp too.

  • Maps

    Free maps such as TopoActive, CourseView and Ski, checked before they are written, and maps of your own.

  • Music

    Music, podcasts and audiobooks, with playlists.

  • Activities

    The activities of the watch and of its backups – sport, time, distance and ascent – exported as GPX.

  • Backups and diagnostics

    Backups of the device and copies of its diagnostic files, kept on your computer; the primary training device and the primary wearable.

  • GTK 4 and libadwaita
  • Command line
  • Sync in the background
  • No telemetry
  • Light and dark style
  • English and Polish

Devices

Tested by the developer on a fēnix 8, an Edge 1050 and a Varia RTL515, and reported working by users on more fēnix, Instinct, Edge, Forerunner and inReach models. Other Garmin watches and bike computers that connect over USB should work too – reports are welcome. What works on each: Supported devices.

Requirements

  • System

    Linux with Flatpak; the GNOME runtime comes from Flathub. Watches that connect over MTP need gvfs with its MTP backend (gvfs-mtp), which GNOME has.

  • Garmin

    A Garmin Connect account, and the Garmin Express installer for Windows: openExpress reads two values from your own copy during setup, so it carries no Garmin code. No Windows needed.

Updates arrive with flatpak update or in GNOME Software. Without the repository, download the .flatpak bundle from Releases; a bundle does not update itself. Other ways to install.

Privacy

  • No telemetry.
  • Your password is typed only on Garmin's page.
  • Sync never deletes files from the watch, and every file written to it is checked.

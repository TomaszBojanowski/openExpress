openExpress

Manage your Garmin device on Linux

An unofficial app for Garmin watches and bike computers: sync with Garmin Connect, software updates, maps, Connect IQ apps and music, over USB.

New in 0.10.0: the Device Status page

Unofficial and independent: not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Garmin.

Install Source on GitHub

Install downloads a file that opens in GNOME Software. Or in a terminal:

flatpak install --user https://flatpak.bojanowski.me/io.github.tomaszbojanowski.OpenExpress.flatpakref
Or add the repository 
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists flathub https://dl.flathub.org/repo/flathub.flatpakrepo
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists bojanowski https://flatpak.bojanowski.me/bojanowski.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user bojanowski io.github.tomaszbojanowski.OpenExpress

Courses

Experimental. Copy a GPX file to the watch or bike computer, and the device turns it into a course when you unplug the cable – no Garmin Connect needed. Before the copy, the file is shown on a map with its name on the device and what the device will do with it; the points of a route are joined with straight lines, not roads, and the confirmation dialog says so. The Courses page lists the courses on the device and where each came from, the files waiting for it and its saved locations. Tried on an Edge 1050 and a fēnix 8.

The courses in the screenshots are made up, around Gdańsk, Sopot and Gdynia.

Activities

Open an activity to see its track on a map, coloured by heart rate, pace or elevation, with a chart of each. Choose a lap to find it on the map and on the chart; below them are the heart rate zones and the training effect as the device worked them out. The map's tiles come from OpenStreetMap only once you turn them on; until then the track is drawn on a plain background. The activities of the device and of its backups are listed together, and each can be saved as a GPX file.

The screenshots show a real hike of mine near Cortina d'Ampezzo, in the Dolomites.

Connect IQ apps

Install, update and remove apps, turn them on or off and put them back in order – also on watches that hide their app folder, such as the fēnix 8. Apps you added in the Connect IQ Store wait on the same page.

Software updates

Updates for the device, downloaded from Garmin and checked before they are written to it.

Device Status

What needs attention on the device: its crashes and software updates, what takes its space, and the files it no longer needs (older memory dumps and updates it did not install), removed only after a confirmation.

What it does

  • Sync with Garmin Connect

    New activities, sleep, HRV and other data go up; courses, workouts and Connect IQ apps queued in Garmin Connect come down to the device. TrueUp too. A device not yet on your account can be added from openExpress (experimental).

  • Maps and courses

    Free maps such as TopoActive, CourseView and Ski, checked by MD5 before they are written, and maps of your own. Courses from GPX files, copied to the device without Garmin Connect (experimental).

  • Music

    Music, podcasts and audiobooks, with playlists.

  • Backups and diagnostics

    Backups of the device and copies of its diagnostic files, kept on your computer. Shows and sets the primary training device and the primary wearable.

Devices

Tested by the developer on a fēnix 8, an Edge 1050 and a Varia RTL515, and reported working by users on more fēnix, Instinct, Edge, Forerunner and inReach models. Other Garmin watches and bike computers that connect over USB should work too – reports are welcome. What works on each: Supported devices.

Requirements

  • System

    Linux with Flatpak; the GNOME runtime comes from Flathub. Devices that connect over MTP need gvfs with its MTP backend (gvfs-mtp), which GNOME has.

  • Garmin

    A Garmin Connect account, and the Garmin Express installer for Windows. openExpress contains no Garmin code; the application key, its secret and the value that signs download addresses are read from your own copy of the installer during setup. No Windows needed.

Current version: 0.10.2. Updates arrive with flatpak update or in GNOME Software. Without the repository, download the .flatpak bundle from Releases; a bundle does not update itself. Other ways to install.

Privacy

  • No telemetry.
  • Your password is typed only on Garmin's page.
  • Sync never deletes files from the device, and every file written to it is checked.
  • The map of an activity asks OpenStreetMap for its streets only after you turn them on.

## USB modes: MTP and mass storage

Devices that connect over MTP (most watches and bike computers, such as the fēnix 8 and the
Edge 1050) work in both the Flatpak and the version from source. openExpress also supports
Garmin devices that connect as a USB drive (mass storage mode: some watches, such as the
Forerunner 55, older models, the inReach Mini 2 and accessories such as the Varia RTL515): their information and software updates, in
the window and on the command line, in the Flatpak and in the version from source. Both find
such a device by themselves. The window gives it a tile of its own, also when the system has
not mounted it yet (as on KDE, until a file manager opens it), and mounts it when you choose
it; when an MTP device is connected too, the command line opens the MTP device. `--path` is only for a folder without a device, such as a copy.
Once its drive is ejected, a watch in this mode switches to its Garmin USB mode, which
openExpress does not use: unplug the cable and plug it in again to use it again. Such a
device is backed up as any other, and a watch is synced both ways, as over MTP: its new files
are uploaded and the files Garmin Connect queues for it (courses, workouts, training plans,
TrueUp) are written to it, with the **Sync** button or `openexpress sync`, never in the
background. A drive is synced when its `GarminDevice.xml` declares data that goes to Garmin
Connect and Garmin's update server answers without saying that Garmin Connect does not take
its data (a Varia declares none; for an inReach Mini 2 the server says Garmin Connect does not
take it); without an answer of that server it is not synced either. Otherwise the device page
and the command say why. Connect IQ apps queued for a drive
stay in Garmin Connect's queue. After a sync that wrote files to it the drive is ejected (the
window asks first), so that the watch takes them. Apps, maps and music are not supported for
such a device; the window and the commands say so. The sync of a drive is new: it was tried
with a pendrive standing in for a watch and is confirmed on an Instinct 2S
([#24](https://github.com/TomaszBojanowski/openExpress/issues/24)). A car or motorcycle
navigator that connects as a USB drive (such as an older nüvi) should therefore get its
software updates, but no map updates. Mass storage is tested by the developer on the Varia RTL515 (also
copying a component update and ejecting the drive in the window), and users
report software updates working on an inReach Mini 2, a Forerunner 55 and a Forerunner 920XT,
and sync on an Instinct 2S (see the table); reports from other devices in mass storage mode are welcome. If you are not sure which mode your device
uses, plug it in: if it appears as a USB drive in your file manager rather than through MTP,
it is in mass storage mode.

A Garmin device that connects with an HID interface and nothing else, or with HID beside a USB
drive (such as the inReach Mini of the first generation, whose drive goes away after about a
minute), offers neither MTP nor a drive that stays: openExpress cannot use it, and the window
and the commands say so at once (*Device Not Supported*).
